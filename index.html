Ziyi Zhang

I'm currently a Machine Learning Engineer at TikTok, working on next-generation recommender systems for TikTok Mall. Previously, I worked on large-scale time series forecasting to optimize TikTok Shop's supply chain operations.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from Texas A&M University, supervised by Prof. Nick Duffield. I received my B.Eng. in Electronic Information Engineering from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC).

My research focuses on sequence modeling, causality, and foundation models, particularly in recommender systems and time series forecasting.
